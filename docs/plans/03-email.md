# PRD 03 — Email

Input to OpenSpec. Do not implement from this file. Depends on students and, for money mail, PRD 02.

Transactional email only at first, via **Resend** (free tier, about 3,000 messages a month):

- Enrolment confirmation
- Lesson reminder (T−24h and T−2h)
- Cancellation and reschedule
- Invoice and receipt
- Phase milestone (“learner licence ready to attest”)
- Quiet hours and language preference (EN/FR)

Later, optional: a weekly office digest (“tomorrow’s roster”) and an instructor digest. Skip SMS until no-shows justify about $0.01–0.05 per message.

No marketing blasts in this PRD. Law 25 purpose limitation: addresses collected for the course are not reused for unrelated promotion without consent.

## Exit

Reminders go out for booked lessons. Every money event has an email trail.
